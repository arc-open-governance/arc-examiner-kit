Single entry command, from the kit root: `python3 run_exam.py`.

Strength, on its face: local record-layer only. A run of the kit
establishes that the claimed record-layer semantics are coherent,
frozen, and reproducible against the fixture implementation shipped
in the kit; it establishes nothing about any deployed system.
